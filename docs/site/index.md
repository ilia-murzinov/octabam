# ilia · octabam

Personal remixes of [octabam](https://github.com/sambanks/octabam) for the
Elektron Octatrack (OS 1.40C): Tim SYNTH / CX OSC, VOID, SHATTER, STUTTR,
RESONATOR, SCOPE, COPY PROJECT, DARK REV kept.

## Get started

1. Read [what's in Ilia's remixes](remix-ilia.md).
2. [Build and install](web-builder.md) from your own 1.40C.
3. Look up a module in the [feature directory](reference/modules.md) or the
   list on the remixes page.

## Unofficial firmware

Octabam is unofficial firmware, not made, endorsed or supported by Elektron.
Installing it is generally safe and can be reverted by reinstalling stock OS
([Safety and Recovery](safety.md)). Elektron is not responsible for any
damage to your Octatrack.

Upstream octabam (Sam's SPECTRUM / CHARACTER / MODULATION image) still has its
[original overview](remix-octabam.md) and [manual](reference/manual/octabam/index.md)
on this fork for reference — that is **not** what the builder ships.
